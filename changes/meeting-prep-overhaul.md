### New
- You can now generate an on-demand, source-cited **Suggested Agenda** in the **Preparation** sidebar panel after enabling **Meeting preparation** in **Settings → AI**, reviewing pre-selected local notes, emails, promises, commitments, and files before anything is sent, and inserting chosen items into your meeting note with **Undo**.
- Each note now has an **Exclude from AI** toggle in **Note Details** (synced across devices and preserved in Markdown export and import) that keeps the note out of AI requests.

### Improved
- Meeting **Preparation** now resolves attendee names from Contacts, displays the calendar invite description, surfaces open promises, shared files, related notes, and carried-over tasks from earlier meetings in the series, promotes multi-attendee threads, and automatically expands attendee mail when no direct threads match.
- **Follow-through** now sits below preparation context before a meeting ends and moves to the top afterward, and **Reserve Prep Time** has been removed.
- In a daily note's sidebar, **Links** now shows the resolved meeting title instead of a bare start time, hides the duplicate **Prepare** button when that meeting is already listed in **Agenda**, and top-aligns row action icons with row content.
