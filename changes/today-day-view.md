### New
- **Today** now has a simpler **Planning inbox** of cards on the left and a larger **Today’s schedule** on the right. **Today's plan** lists what you chose to do today, focus first, and says how many times an item has been carried over; check items off, **Book** the next free half hour within your working hours, or remove them. Add to it from **Suggested**, **Also Due**, **Tasks**, **You Said You Would**, **Keep in Touch**, **Starred Mail**, **Goals**, and **Notes**, or drag a card onto the day to book time and plan it at once. The plan is kept in today's daily note.
- Today's schedule opens on your working hours with the time outside them shaded, and dims finished and declined meetings. Use a meeting’s note icon to open its notes or start an entry in its daily note, then prepare alongside the note editor; **Day details** holds the mail and meeting summaries, and **Review Today** looks ahead to tomorrow.
