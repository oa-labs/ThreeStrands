### New
- **Today** now has a simpler **Planning Inbox** on the left and a larger **Today’s Schedule** on the right. Filter one list with **All**, **Tasks**, **Emails**, and **Contacts**; note and goal tasks appear under Tasks, and scheduled items move to the calendar without completing their source.
- Today's schedule opens on your working hours with the time outside them shaded, and dims finished and declined meetings. Use a meeting’s note icon to open its notes or start an entry in its daily note, then prepare alongside the note editor; **Day Details** holds the mail and meeting summaries, and **Review Today** looks ahead to tomorrow.

### Fixed
- Meeting preparation now receives keyboard focus when opened, returns focus to its event link when closed, and leaves Escape available when its saved session is not visible. Today’s filter buttons and labels follow the app’s shared typography and capitalization.
