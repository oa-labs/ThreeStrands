### New
- **Today** is now your plan beside a view of today's calendar. **Today's plan** lists what you chose to do today, focus first, and says how many times an item has been carried over; check items off, **Book** the next free half hour within your working hours, or remove them. Add to it from **Suggested**, **Also Due**, **Tasks**, **You Said You Would**, **Keep in Touch**, **Starred Mail**, **Goals**, and **Notes**, or drag a card onto the day to book time and plan it at once. The plan is kept in today's daily note.
- Today's day view opens on your working hours with the time outside them shaded, and dims finished and declined meetings. Meetings say what is ready for them and how much unread mail their attendees sent; choosing one shows its preparation in place of the plan. A one-line **Mail** summary in the header replaces the unread-mail column, listing invitations and counting what needs a reply, with **Open Inbox** for the rest.
