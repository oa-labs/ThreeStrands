### New
- **Today** is now your plan beside a view of today's calendar. **Today's plan** lists what you chose to do today, focus first; check items off, **Book** the next free half hour, or remove them. Add to it from **Also Due**, **Tasks**, **You Said You Would**, and **Keep in Touch**, or drag a card onto the day to book time and plan it at once. The plan is kept in today's daily note.
- Meetings in Today's day view say what is ready for them and how much unread mail their attendees sent; choosing one shows its preparation in place of the plan. A one-line **Mail** summary in the header replaces the unread-mail column, listing invitations and counting what needs a reply, with **Open Inbox** for the rest.
