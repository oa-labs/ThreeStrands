### Improved
- The **Agenda** section in a daily note's sidebar now has a calendar filter so you can choose which calendars to show there, remembered until you change it.
- Each meeting in a daily note's **Agenda** now offers a **Prepare** button right next to **Add Note**, so you can open meeting preparation directly from the agenda.
- The **Preparation** sidebar panel now separates the meeting title, time, and attendees into a cleaner summary and moves **Review Follow-through** and **Draft Follow-up** out of the cramped section heading into the **Follow-through** section body.
