### New

- **Inbox Assistants** in **Agents** help clear aging email with configurable enable/disable controls, age thresholds, and optional daily or weekly schedules. **Stale Reads Summarizer** presents key takeaways and deadlines for older newsletters and long unread messages so you can archive them, save to **Notes**, or create a task; **Expired & Already-Handled Sweeper** flags emails whose linked tasks are complete or whose dates or follow-ups have passed; and **Aging Reply Drafter** prepares review-before-send reply drafts (and gentle check-ins on older threads) for unanswered conversations.
