### New
- **Write Email** on a task in **Today** opens a message that finishes the task once it is sent, and the task then shows which email finished it as **Done by email**. On a check-in, it opens a new message to the contact. After you send or schedule a message, ThreeStrands offers to mark the one task that clearly matches it done, and does nothing until you click **Mark Done**.

### Fixed
- Undoing a send no longer moves a repeating follow-up forward; it advances when the message actually leaves.
