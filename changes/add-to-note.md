### New
- Press <kbd>W</kbd> or choose **⋯ → Add to Note…** while reading a conversation to copy a passage of an email, a link to it, and its attachments and images into any note, including an upcoming meeting's note. This replaces **Add to Today's Note**, which is now one of the notes you can pick.

### Fixed
- If **Add to Note** fails while creating a meeting note, it tells you when it also couldn't undo the meeting note setup.
