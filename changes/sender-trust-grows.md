### Improved
- Trusting a sender now remembers the sites their links open, and each time you trust a message it adds any new sites, so a trusted sender no longer warns every time they link somewhere new you've already approved.
- Trust for a sender's link sites now lasts a year from when you last confirmed it, after which the app asks again.
- The "Different delivery pattern" warning now names the new sites or the different address instead of a general message.
