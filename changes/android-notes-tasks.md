### New
- ThreeStrands for Android lets you write Daily Notes, search and edit notes, and manage tasks offline. Pair it with desktop through an HTTPS S3 sync connector to keep your Notes and Tasks in step.

### Improved
- Android uses the desktop accent colors and day-to-night backdrop. Daily Notes, Notes, and Tasks have simpler controls, and switching tabs remembers your place.
- Android note previews and checklists are easier to scan. Undo is available after completing a task or moving a note to Trash.

### Fixed
- ThreeStrands for Android keeps the header and navigation tabs clear of the system bars.
- Long note titles wrap on Android, and the note body and bottom tabs stay within the screen.
