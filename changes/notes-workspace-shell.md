### New
- **Notes** (<kbd>4</kbd>) keeps a note for each day and notes of your own, written in Markdown and saved as you type. With cross-device sync on, notes reach your other devices; update every device before you start writing.

### Improved
- The view shortcuts follow the sidebar order: <kbd>5</kbd> now opens Contacts, <kbd>6</kbd> Files, and <kbd>7</kbd> Agents.
