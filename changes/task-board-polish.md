### New
- Drag cards up and down on the task board to put them in your own order, or press Option-↑/↓ (Alt-↑/↓ on Windows and Linux) to move the selected card. Your order syncs across your devices.

### Improved
- Task board cards now have a status button next to the title. Click it, or right-click the card, to move the task to any column in one step, replacing the per-card move buttons.
- The **Tasks** heading now counts open tasks and always matches the **All** count. Done and cancelled tasks no longer show a due date, and the email icon names the conversation a task came from.
- Task details are tidier. Source conversations and related emails share one **Conversations** list, notes have their own **Notes** section, and **Repeat** is a simple choice of Never, Daily, Weekly, every 2 or 4 weeks, or a custom number of days.
- Search and the view filters now share one row at the top of **Tasks**.
