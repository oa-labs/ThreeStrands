### New
- **Prepare** for a meeting from its details in **Calendar**, an agenda, **Today**, the command palette, or by pressing <kbd>p</kbd>. Preparation shows that meeting's notes, notes from earlier meetings in its series, mail about the meeting or with several of its attendees, and open commitments, each one click from its source, with **Back to Meeting** to return. It reads only what is on your computer and changes nothing; <kbd>Escape</kbd> closes it.
- **Today** lists the meetings still ahead with what is ready for each. Choosing one shows its preparation beside the agenda, and the arrow keys move to the next or previous meeting. **Reserve Prep Time** adds a "Prepare for" task under the meeting in that day's note, and **No Prep Needed** sets a meeting aside on this computer.
