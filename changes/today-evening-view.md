### New
- **Settings → Appearance → Today Backdrop** adds an optional sky in a header aligned with Today's panels, with a matching tint in the side gutters and between panels that fades down the page: daytime from 6 am, twilight from 6 pm, and a dark night sky with stars from 9 pm. The date and title have space around them and stay legible over darker skies; the backdrop is off by default and stays on this computer.
