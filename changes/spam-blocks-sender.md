### New
- **Mark Spam** now also blocks the sender, so their future mail goes straight to Spam. Blocked senders who stop emailing are forgotten after six months. Review or unblock them in **Settings → Muted Senders**.
  **Not spam** on a conversation in Spam, from its toolbar, the batch actions, the sender warning, or <kbd>!</kbd>, moves it back to the Inbox and unblocks its sender, and **Undo** blocks them again.
