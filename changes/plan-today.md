### New
- **Plan Today** walks you through your day in five steps: review what you finished and what you didn't get to (carry, defer, or drop each one), choose what today is for from your sources, a search, or a new task, prepare for your meetings, book time for your choices on the day's calendar, and save the plan to today's daily note in one step with **Undo**. Start it from Today's header, where **Edit Plan** reopens a saved plan, or from the command palette. Late in the day, **Plan Tomorrow** plans the next day the same way, and choices you haven't saved are kept if you close the app.
