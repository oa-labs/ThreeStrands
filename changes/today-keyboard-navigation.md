### Improved
- Navigate Today's **Planning Inbox** from the keyboard: switch filter tabs with <kbd>←</kbd> / <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>[</kbd> / <kbd>]</kbd>, move across items with <kbd>j</kbd> / <kbd>k</kbd> or <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd>, clear selection with <kbd>Esc</kbd>, open items with <kbd>Enter</kbd> or their source with <kbd>o</kbd>, complete with <kbd>e</kbd>, schedule with <kbd>s</kbd>, add to today's plan with <kbd>a</kbd>, remove from the plan or dismiss with <kbd>#</kbd>, and jump to quick-add with <kbd>d</kbd>.
- Press <kbd>t</kbd> in **Today** to return to today's date and <kbd>b</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> to create a new event.

### Fixed
- Pressing <kbd>d</kbd> or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> outside **Mail** no longer acts on the last selected email conversation in the background.
