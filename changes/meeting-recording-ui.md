### New
- On a Mac, ThreeStrands can record a meeting's microphone and your computer's audio from the menu bar, a meeting's details, or Today, stopping cleanly if your Mac goes to sleep. Turn it on and choose your microphone in **Settings → Meeting Recording → Recording & Menu Bar**; saved audio is compressed after transcription, stays on your Mac, and follows your retention choice.
- With **Offer to Record Meetings** on in **Settings → Meeting Recording → Recording & Menu Bar**, the menu bar shows a three-strands icon that offers the current or upcoming meeting before it begins, badges when a conferencing app or in-meeting browser uses your microphone so unscheduled calls are easy to start, and gently nudges if a recording continues silently after a call ends.
- Recordings are transcribed on your Mac using **Apple On-Device** on macOS 26 and later or downloadable Whisper models (**Whisper Fast** and **Whisper Accurate**) in **Settings → Meeting Recording → Transcription & Vocabulary**, with a **Transcription Language** choice, a **Custom Vocabulary** list supporting comma-separated entry and **Suggest from Sent Mail** (excluding your own name and email signature, with suggestions unselected by default), and a switch in **Transcribe During Meetings** to run during the meeting or only after you stop.
- Optionally turn on **Cloud Transcription** in **Settings → Meeting Recording → Transcription & Vocabulary** (after reviewing the privacy notice) to transcribe or **Re-transcribe with Cloud** from **Show Transcript** using OpenRouter (such as Microsoft's MAI-Transcribe-2 model with remote speaker labels) or an OpenAI-compatible audio provider within your daily AI budget.
- Finished recordings can be reviewed from the menu bar, a meeting's recordings, or **Review Last Recording**: add structured meeting notes, decisions, tasks, actions, and open questions, attach the full Markdown transcript to the note, and default to creating a dedicated meeting note linked from your daily note (including for ad-hoc recordings).
- When an AI provider is configured, you can summarize a recording transcript into scannable topic-grouped meeting notes and grounded suggestions (turning **Include My Notes** on per session for notes not excluded from AI, or enabling automatic summaries and customizing **Summary Instructions** across devices in **Settings → Meeting Recording → Summaries**).
