### New
- On a Mac, ThreeStrands can record a meeting's microphone and your computer's audio from the menu bar, a meeting's details, or Today, stopping cleanly if your Mac goes to sleep. Turn it on and choose your microphone in **Settings → Meeting Recording**; saved audio is compressed after transcription, stays on your Mac, and follows your retention choice.
- With **Offer to Record Meetings** on in **Settings → Meeting Recording**, the menu bar offers the current or upcoming meeting before it begins, shows a badge when a conferencing app or in-meeting browser uses your microphone so unscheduled calls are easy to start, and gently nudges if a recording continues silently after a call ends.
- Recordings are transcribed on your Mac using **Apple On-Device** on macOS 26 and later or downloadable Whisper models (**Fast** and **Accurate**) in **Settings → Meeting Recording**, with a **Transcription Language** choice, a **Custom Vocabulary** list you can seed with **Suggest from Sent Mail**, and a switch in **Transcribe During Meetings** to run during the meeting or only after you stop.
- Optionally turn on **Cloud Transcription** in **Settings → Meeting Recording** (after reviewing the privacy notice) to transcribe or **Re-transcribe with Cloud** from **Show Transcript** using OpenRouter (such as Microsoft's MAI-Transcribe-2 model with remote speaker labels) or an OpenAI-compatible audio provider within your daily AI budget.
- Finished recordings can be reviewed from the menu bar, a meeting's recordings, or **Review Last Recording**: add a **Summary**, decisions, tasks, actions, and open questions, attach the full Markdown transcript to the note, and save ad-hoc recordings to your daily note or match them to a meeting.
- When an AI provider is configured, you can optionally summarize a recording transcript into grounded suggestions (turning **Include My Notes** on per session for notes not excluded from AI, or enabling automatic summaries in **Settings → Meeting Recording**).
