A Closer Look

Everything Where You Need It

  1. 01

    One Inbox for Every Account

    Read and triage multiple Gmail accounts in one merged view, or focus on one. Archive, star, label, and process conversations in batches, with undo for every reversible action.

  2. 02

    Split Inboxes

    Route high-volume senders into their own tabs by domain, label, or address pattern. The main inbox stays focused on people.

  3. 03

    Compose Without Friction

    Rich text, attachments, and reusable snippets. Every send gets a ten-second undo window, and a durable outbox survives restarts.

  4. 04

    Tasks Tied to Conversations

    Standalone tasks and conversation-linked actions, follow-ups, and waiting-fors with due dates and repeats. Linked follow-ups can complete when a new reply lands.

  5. 05

    Today's Schedule at a Glance

    Press T to see your day beside your inbox. Check free times against your calendars and working hours, then put them in a reply.

  6. 06

    Plan the Week

    A full week view across the calendars you choose. Open a time slot or select New event to start planning without leaving your mail.

  7. 07

    From Conversation to Calendar

    Create events with invitees from the week view or a reviewed meeting suggestion. Check the title, time, and calendar before creating.

  8. 08

    People, in Context

    Browse, add, and edit contacts with multiple addresses, notes, and correspondence history. The conversation context panel brings people, related tasks, and meetings beside your mail. Optional AI enrichment suggests profile details for you to apply.

ThreeStrands

Local-First

Sealed on Your Device

ThreeStrands talks to Gmail directly. There's no ThreeStrands account to create and no ThreeStrands server in the middle — so there's nothing of yours for us to store, scan, or lose.

Inspect the source to see how the app handles your mail and credentials. You can build, use, and modify it for personal or internal business use under the PolyForm Perimeter License 1.0.1. The license prohibits providing others with a competing product built from this software, even for free.

Your Computer ThreeStrands + a local SQLite cache
Gmail Google's API, over TLS
ThreeStrands Servers There are none.

  • Works Offline

    Mail is cached in SQLite for instant search and reading. Changes and sends queue locally and resume when you reconnect.

  • Keys in Your Keychain

    OAuth credentials live in your operating system's credential store — never in a plain file, never in someone else's cloud.

  • Encrypted Settings Export

    Move preferences, account display details, saved contacts, snippets, and Split Inboxes with a password-encrypted file. Mail, drafts, queued sends, and credentials stay behind.

  • Encrypted Sync Beta

    Sync tasks, saved contacts, snippets, Split Inboxes, portable preferences, and account display details with end-to-end encryption through a shared folder, S3-compatible storage, or IPFS. Mail, drafts, queued sends, and provider credentials stay on each device.

Optional AI

Assistance, with You in Control

Bring your own provider and API key for summaries, drafting help, contact enrichment, and suggested tasks or meetings. Ask questions about a conversation, search other mail when you choose, or share selected readable attachments. You review and apply tasks, calendar events, contact changes, and drafts. You choose when to send.

  • Your provider, your key.
  • Chat searches other mail only when you choose Search all mail. You select which readable attachments to share.
  • AI starts off. Set up your provider and key, then enable the features you want.

Proactive briefs are a separate opt-in: when enabled, qualifying conversations are sent to your provider after you stay on them. Contact enrichment reviews correspondence with that person when you request it.

Track requests, tokens, and estimated costs in settings. An optional fast model handles summaries, reply drafts, and contact enrichment while your main model handles briefs, suggestions, and chat.

Roadmap

Where It's Going

Directions for unfinished work — not dated commitments. Everything above ships today; everything here is next.

  1. Snooze and Scheduled Sending

    Put a conversation aside until a chosen time, and queue a message for later delivery.

  2. Sending Aliases and Signatures

    Send from verified Gmail aliases, with a signature for each identity.

  3. Drafts Across Devices

    Start a reply on one computer and finish it on another, with clear controls over draft synchronization.

  4. Connected Address Books

    Import existing Google Contacts and keep them connected to your local profiles, notes, and correspondence history.

  5. Smarter Inbox Rules

    Inspectable, user-controlled classification beyond domain, label, and address rules.

  6. More Mail Providers

    Microsoft 365, then IMAP and SMTP. Gmail is the only provider today.

  7. Encrypted Sync Reliability

    Harden the encrypted sync beta across devices, especially upgrades and interrupted joins.

  8. Shared and Mobile Workflows

    A mobile companion and shared conversations or drafts, where demand justifies them.
