Three Strands, One Workflow

Mail, Schedule, and Notes — Tied Together by Tasks

Most email clients stop at the message. ThreeStrands keeps your calendar and your notes right beside the conversation, and every follow-up runs through all three, so nothing slips between apps.

Mail

A Calm, Fast Inbox

Bring Gmail and IMAP accounts into one view, split high-volume mail into its own tabs, and triage in batches with undo.

Schedule

Your Day, Beside Your Mail

Press 0 for Today: your agenda, unread mail, tasks due, and the things you said you would do, on one screen. See your day or week from Google Calendar, find free time against your working hours, and create or edit events, answer invitations, or put available times in a reply.

Notes

Notes That Know the Meeting

Press 4 for Notes: a note for each day and notes of your own, written in Markdown. Add a meeting to that day's note with links to the event and everyone invited, link notes with [[ and people or emails with @, and find every link again from the other side.

Tasks · What Ties Them Together

One Task List Running Through All Three

Turn a conversation into an action, follow-up, or waiting-for that closes itself when the reply arrives. Write a task in a meeting note and check it off in Tasks. Drag a task onto your week to book the time for it, and roll each one up to a quarterly or yearly goal.

A Closer Look

Everything Where You Need It

Connect Gmail or an IMAP/SMTP account, including Proton Mail through Proton Mail Bridge. Google Calendar connects separately and works alongside either mail provider. CalDAV and CardDAV connections are not yet available.

  1. 01

    One Inbox for Every Account

    Read and triage Gmail and IMAP accounts in one merged view, or focus on one. Give accounts nicknames and colors so work and personal mail stay easy to recognize. Archive, star, label, and process conversations in batches, with undo for every reversible action. Snooze a conversation until later with h, then reschedule it or return it early from Snoozed. Snoozed mail leaves Inbox in Gmail and IMAP too; it returns when ThreeStrands is running and connected, or when a new reply arrives.

  2. 02

    Split Inboxes and Saved Searches

    Route high-volume senders into their own tabs by domain, label, or address pattern. The main inbox stays focused on people. Starred conversations gather in their own Starred tab, read and archived mail included.

    Save a search with a name, one account or all of them, and quick filters, then open it as a tab beside your Split Inboxes. Saved searches stay on this computer.

  3. 03

    Less Noise from Newsletters

    See a newsletter sender’s recent emails beside the message. Unsubscribe, archive their inbox mail, or create a Split Inbox from the same panel. Mute marks future mail from that sender read and archives it automatically, across your devices when encrypted sync is enabled.

  4. 04

    Write with the Context You Need

    Write with rich text, inline images, attachments, reusable snippets, and formatted signatures with logos. Choose separate signature defaults for new messages and replies, or import your signature from Gmail. Keep recipient history, shared files, related tasks, meetings, and available times beside your draft. After you select someone, recipient suggestions prioritize people you often email together, using history kept on your computer without AI. Groups expand into individual recipients. Choose a verified Gmail alias in Sending Address; replies use the address the original message was sent to, and each sending address can have its own signature defaults.

    Local checks flag missing attachments, likely address typos, account mismatches, and group addresses exposed in To or Cc. Optional AI draft review offers specific feedback against the outcome you want. Drafts save to your Gmail or IMAP Drafts folder, so you can finish them on your phone, the web, or another computer. Send now includes a ten-second undo window, and a durable outbox survives restarts. Sent keeps your outgoing conversations together, including messages sent from other devices. Edit as New reuses a sent message and its attachments for new recipients.

  5. 05

    Tasks Tied to Conversations

    Standalone tasks and conversation-linked actions, follow-ups, and waiting-fors with due dates and repeats, on a list or a board. Open any task to edit it as you go, step through tasks with j and k, and press o to jump to its conversation. Linked follow-ups can complete when a new reply lands. Need time to do one? Drag it from Open tasks onto your calendar week to book 30 minutes.

  6. 06

    Goals Your Tasks Roll Up To

    Set goals for the year, half, and quarter in each account, and link tasks to the goal they move forward. Select a goal to see only its work, with open and done counts and a flag when nothing has moved in three weeks. At the end of a period, review each goal and mark it achieved, drop it, or carry it forward.

  7. 07

    Your Day at a Glance

    Press 0 for Today. One line sums up your day: how many meetings, when you’re free until, and when you’re double-booked. Below it: your agenda with a Join button for the next meeting and overlaps marked, unread mail sorted into Invitations, Needs a reply, From people, and Updates, and tasks that are overdue or due today. A Now line dims what’s finished, tomorrow’s first events wait below, and people due for a Keep in Touch check-in appear beside your tasks. You said you would lists promises from your own sent mail, like “I’ll send the report over,” that you haven’t followed up on yet. Start the app on Today if you like.

    When the inbox is clear, Inbox zero shows when the account was last checked, so you can tell a clear inbox from a connection problem, and links straight to Today.

  8. 08

    Find a Time, Right in the Conversation

    Find Times offers free times beside the conversation. With optional AI, a request to meet next week finds times next week, and when someone offers a few times, each one is checked against your calendars. Select the ones you want and draft a reply, or insert them into the message you are writing. Writing to coworkers? Include them, and their calendars are checked too. Working hours can leave a lunch gap, a Drive Time Buffer reserves travel time around events with a location, and separate profiles keep different hours for work and personal accounts. Match a profile to lunch, breakfast, dinner, or coffee invitations, and Find Times uses those hours instead. Each offered time is marked when it falls back-to-back with another meeting or leaves only a short break.

  9. 09

    Plan the Week

    A full week view across the Google calendars you choose. Customize calendar colors, show or hide accounts, and open a time slot or select New event to start planning without leaving your mail. Open tasks wait beside the week; drag one onto the calendar to book time for it, or check it off there when it is done, with Undo if needed.

  10. 10

    From Conversation to Calendar

    Create events with invitees from the week view or a reviewed meeting suggestion. Check the title, time, location, and calendar before creating, or mark the event All day. When a newsletter announces several events, Get Brief and conversation chat can offer each one at its stated date and time. Edit or delete events you manage, or Reschedule a meeting you organize with guests’ busy times beside yours and suggested times within your working hours. Invite People suggests your contacts and contact groups as you type, ranked by how often you email them, and Location suggests places from your other events.

    Invitations arrive as a card beside the email: Accept, Maybe, or Decline with your current answer clearly marked, or suggest a different time and review the reply before it goes. Event details show who is going, not going, or hasn’t answered yet.

  11. 11

    A Note for Every Day

    Press 4 for Notes. Each day has its own note, beside a calendar that marks the days you wrote in and lists that day's events. Write in Markdown and it formats as you type, using the same formatting shortcuts as email. Step through days with - and =, or press t for today.

    Notes save as you type and hold pictures and files you paste or drag in, which Files lists too. Import a Reflect folder or a single Markdown file, export everything to a folder of Markdown files, and with encrypted sync on, notes reach your other devices.

  12. 12

    Meeting Notes, Linked Both Ways

    Add Note on a calendar event, in Calendar or Today, writes a line about the meeting under Meetings in that day's note, linking the event and each person invited. Choose Create backlinked note to keep one running note that every meeting with that name shares.

    Open the event later and its notes are right there. A recurring meeting lists the notes from every other meeting in its series, newest first, and a contact's details list the notes that mention them.

  13. 13

    Link Anything to Anything

    Type [[ to link another note, or @ to link a contact or an email conversation by name, address, or subject. Every note lists the notes that link to it, showing each linking line as you would read it. Round tasks you write in a note appear in Tasks and can be checked off in either place.

    Notes search leaves Trash out unless you ask, and ⌘K finds notes alongside commands.

  14. 14

    People, in Context

    Browse, add, and edit contacts with multiple addresses, notes, correspondence history, and shared files. Organize people into groups and start a message to everyone with Email Group. Remember birthdays and recurring Keep in Touch reminders, and bring your address book with CSV or vCard import and export. On macOS, connect selected address books already configured in Apple Contacts, including iCloud. Access stays read-only, and you choose which books contribute contacts and recipient suggestions.

    Groups show recent conversations with their current members, including sent mail, so you can find other people to add. Add Members prioritizes people you often email together using local history. Merge selected profiles to bring someone's addresses and history together, and control which addresses appear in recipient suggestions. The conversation context panel names the person whose history you are viewing and follows the message you are reading. Switch to another participant to see their files and recent emails alongside related tasks and meetings. Optional AI enrichment suggests profile details for you to apply.

  15. 15

    Find the File Without Hunting Down the Email

    Bring attachments from your synced mail together in one Files view, including archived conversations, alongside the pictures and files in your notes. Search by filename or sender, or filter by sender, file type, direction, account, and date. Star the files you return to, and jump straight to the source conversation when you need the context.

    On macOS and Windows, preview a selected document or picture before opening it. Risky attachments show a warning and are never previewed. Mark Spam removes a message’s files from Files, with Undo if needed. Press 6 to open Files. Stars stay on this computer and do not keep an offline copy.

ThreeStrands

Local-First

Your Mail, Under Your Control

ThreeStrands connects directly to your mail providers. There's no ThreeStrands account to create and no ThreeStrands server in the middle — so there's nothing of yours for us to store, scan, or lose.

Inspect the source to see how the app handles your mail and credentials. You can build, use, and modify it for personal or internal business use under the PolyForm Perimeter License 1.0.1. The license prohibits providing others with a competing product built from this software, even for free.

Your Computer ThreeStrands + a local SQLite cache
Your Mail Provider Gmail API or IMAP/SMTP, over TLS
ThreeStrands Servers There are none.

  • Works Offline

    Mail is cached in SQLite for instant search and reading. Changes and immediate sends queue locally and resume when you reconnect. Missed scheduled sends wait for your review.

  • Keys in Your Keychain

    Google sign-in tokens and IMAP/SMTP passwords live in your operating system's credential store. Mail credentials never enter settings exports or encrypted sync.

  • Encrypted Settings Export

    Move preferences, account display details, saved contacts and groups, snippets, signatures and their defaults, muted senders, and Split Inboxes with a password-encrypted file. Mail, drafts, queued sends, and credentials stay behind. Notes stay behind too; export them as Markdown from Settings → Notes.

  • Encrypted Sync

    Sync tasks, goals, saved contacts and groups, snippets, signatures and their defaults, muted senders, snooze schedules, Split Inboxes, portable preferences, and account display details with end-to-end encryption through a shared folder, S3-compatible storage, or IPFS. Drafts travel through your Gmail or IMAP Drafts folder, attachments included. Scheduled-send summaries share the account, subject, timing, sending computer, and last reported status. Mail, attachment files, queued send payloads, and provider credentials stay on each device. Scheduled bodies, recipients, and attachments never sync. Notes sync to your other devices too. Remove a mail account and everything ThreeStrands kept for it on your computer goes too, including drafts, Outbox messages, opened attachments, and older backups; Remove on all devices clears the same everywhere.

Optional AI

Assistance, with You in Control

Bring your own provider and API key for summaries, drafting help, draft review, contact enrichment, and suggested tasks or meetings. Ask questions about a conversation, search other mail when you choose, or share selected readable attachments. You review and apply tasks, calendar events, contact changes, and drafts. You choose when to send.

  • Your provider, your key.
  • Chat searches other mail only when you choose Search all mail. You select which readable attachments to share.
  • AI starts off. Set up your provider and key, then enable the features you want.

Proactive briefs are a separate opt-in: when enabled, qualifying conversations are sent to your provider after you stay on them. Each brief covers only what’s new since your last reply or brief, and says how many messages it shared and with which provider. Contact enrichment reviews correspondence with that person when you request it.

Draft review sends your subject, written text, and any goal you add when you click Review, along with the writing profile you set for that account: a few paragraphs about you and how you want drafts reviewed. Recipient fields, separately stored quoted history, and attachments are excluded, and What was sent shows exactly what went to your provider. Review suggested wording before applying it; revisions can be undone.

Delegate read-only web research in Agents, with its own Anthropic and Exa keys set up in Settings → Web Research. Review the public search subjects before research starts, ask follow-up questions, and save report revisions with their sources. Pause All stops delegated work until you resume it.

Track requests, tokens, and estimated costs in settings. An Activity log lists which feature sent each request, for which conversation, and how many messages; email text is never kept there. An optional fast model handles summaries, reply drafts, and contact enrichment while your main model handles draft review, briefs, suggestions, and chat.

Considered Throughout

The Details Add Up

  • Send Later

    Pick a date, time, and timezone. Reschedule or return to draft before sending starts. With encrypted sync enabled, other computers can see the schedule and its last reported status.

    Sends from the computer where you scheduled it. ThreeStrands must be running, and that computer must be awake, online, and authorized. Missed times wait for your review; another computer never takes over.

  • Undo Send

    Send now gives you ten seconds to change your mind.

  • Durable Outbox

    Queued mail survives restarts and flaky connections.

  • Batch Triage

    Select many, act once, and undo if you need to.

  • Local + Provider Search

    Search cached mail instantly, and reach beyond the cache through your connected provider where supported.

  • Multiple Accounts

    Gmail and IMAP accounts in one merged inbox, with nicknames and colors to tell them apart.

  • Before You Send

    Local checks flag likely mistakes in attachments, recipients, and sender accounts. Review them and decide what to change; no AI required.

  • Open Mail and Invitations

    Mailto links open a draft. On macOS, Windows, and Linux, choose ThreeStrands as your default mail app and open .ics invitations to review or add to Google Calendar.

  • Drafts That Follow You

    Drafts save to your Gmail or IMAP server and come back from it, attachments included. Discard by mistake? Undo brings them back.

  • Snippets

    Reusable replies, a keystroke away.

  • Newsletter Control

    Unsubscribe with confirmation, archive a sender’s inbox mail, or give it a Split Inbox. Mute marks new mail from that sender read and archives it as it arrives; manage your choices in Settings → Muted Senders.

  • Clean Threads

    Repeated quoted replies fold away, and search skips them too.

  • Sent to You, or Cc'd?

    Message headers read “to David Martinez, cc me,” so you can tell at a glance.

  • Right Where You Left Off

    The app reopens to the view you quit in, each account remembers the conversation you had open, and a long email you were partway through picks up where you left off.

  • Readable Mail

    Zoom email on its own, set a minimum font size for tiny sender text, and let hard-to-read sender colors adjust just enough to read. Switch light and dark mode anytime with ⌘D.

  • Read on Your Terms

    Mark conversations read immediately, after a delay, or never when you open them, and choose what archiving opens next. Settings → Reading & Triage.

  • Panels Your Size

    Drag list and side panels wider or narrower. Hide the side panel with \; each view remembers.

  • Self-Healing Cache

    Automatic backups and startup recovery keep your local data safe.
