## Read and Organize {#read-and-organize}

First [connect a mail account](getting-started.html). Open **Mail** in the sidebar or press <kbd>1</kbd>. Select a conversation to read it. Press <kbd>j</kbd> and <kbd>k</kbd> to move between conversations when no text field has focus.

1. Open a conversation you have finished with.
2. Choose **Archive**, or press <kbd>e</kbd>. Archiving removes it from Inbox but keeps it in your mail account.
3. Click **Undo** in the notice if you archived the wrong conversation. You can also find it in **All Mail**.

## Reply and Keep a Draft {#reply-and-keep-a-draft}

1. Choose **Reply** on a message, or press <kbd>r</kbd>.
2. Write your reply. Check **From** when you have several accounts.
3. Press <kbd>Escape</kbd> to close and keep the draft, or choose **Send** when ready.

Drafts save automatically; [Writing Mail](compose.html#start-a-message) explains provider Drafts and conflict choices. A normal send has a ten-second **Undo Send** window.

## Keep a Follow-up {#keep-a-follow-up}

Open a conversation and choose **+ Add task** in its Tasks section, or press <kbd>d</kbd>. Choose an **Action**, **Follow Up**, or **Waiting for Reply**. Find it again in **Tasks**, or press <kbd>3</kbd>. Tasks are separate from archived mail: archiving a conversation does not finish its task.

## Find Your Way Back {#find-your-way-back}

Open **Today**, or press <kbd>0</kbd>, for your agenda, unread mail, and open tasks. Calendar connection is optional and separate from mail. AI is also optional; neither is needed to read, write, search, or create tasks.

Open the command palette with <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + k</kbd> to find actions, including **Help and Documentation**. The **Getting Started** checklist can be reopened there.
