## Create a Task {#create-a-task}

- **From a conversation** — press <kbd>d</kbd>, or click **+ Add Task…** in the conversation's Tasks section. The task stays linked to the conversation.
- **On its own** — press <kbd>3</kbd> for Tasks and click **Add Task…** (or press <kbd>d</kbd>).
- **From a promise** — **Today** lists things you said you would do in your sent mail under **Emails** and **Commitments**; turn one into a task from there.
- **In a note** — type `/Task` in a [note](notes.html#write) for a round task. It appears in **Tasks**, and checking it off in either place checks it off in both. **Open Note** in its details opens the note.
- **On Today** — type into **Add a task** at the end of the [Planning Inbox](today.html#plan-your-day).

Use **Description** for any detail. To write about a task at length, click **Add Note** in its details to start a note linked to it, or type `@` and the task's name in any note to link it.

Each task has a **Type**:

- **Action** — something you need to do.
- **Follow Up** — something to chase. Choose a **Repeat** schedule (**Never**, **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Every 2 weeks**, **Every 4 weeks**, or **Custom…**).
- **Waiting for Reply** — something you're waiting on from someone else.

A **Waiting for Reply** task that's linked to a conversation completes on its own when a new reply arrives. For conversation-linked tasks, **Draft Follow-Up** or **Write Email** opens compose with **Done by email** enabled so sending can complete the task or offer a **Mark Done** notice.

Tasks added from notes stay visible even when you are filtering by a single mail account.

## Work Through Tasks {#work-through-tasks}

Press <kbd>v</kbd> to switch between **List** and **Board**. The board has To Do, In Progress, and Done columns; drag cards or press <kbd>Option + ↑</kbd>/<kbd>↓</kbd> (<kbd>Alt + ↑</kbd>/<kbd>↓</kbd> on Windows and Linux) to set manual card order. The **Completed** filter is available in **List** view (use **Show N older completed** when needed). Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift + Tab</kbd> to cycle through filters (**All**, **Overdue**, **Today**, **Upcoming**, **Anytime**, **Waiting**, and **Completed** in List), and use the single-row search and filter bar to narrow tasks. The heading shows the count of open tasks.

Use a card's status button or right-click menu to move it between statuses or delete it. Task details save automatically as you edit them. Pull down on the list to refresh it.

To set aside time for a task, drag it from **Open tasks** in **Calendar** onto the week. See [Book Time for Tasks](calendar.html#book-time-for-tasks).

## Goals {#goals}

<ts-shot scene="goals" alt="Goals for the quarter, each with open and done task counts."></ts-shot>

Goals give your tasks a reason. Open them with **Goals** or <kbd>g</kbd> then <kbd>k</kbd>, filter by **All tasks** or **No goal**, press <kbd>Escape</kbd> or click outside to close the picker, and click **Add goal**:

- **Horizon** — Quarter, Half, or Year.
- **Period** — such as Q4 2026, H2 2026, or 2026.
- **Supports** — optionally, a longer-term goal this one feeds.

Press <kbd>Shift + g</kbd> on a task to link it to a goal. Select a goal to see only its work, with open and done counts. A goal is marked **Stale** when none of its tasks have moved in three weeks.

## Review a Period {#review-a-period}

When a period ends, ThreeStrands asks you to review its goals. Mark each one **Achieved** or **Dropped**, or **carry it** into the next period. Choose **Later** to put the review off for the rest of the quarter.
