ThreeStrands is a keyboard-first desktop email client (with an [Android companion app](android.html)) that keeps your follow-ups, notes, and schedule beside your mail. Everything runs on your computer or phone: there is no ThreeStrands account or server.

## Compatibility at a Glance {#compatibility-at-a-glance}

Mail connects through Google sign-in, Gmail with an app password, or IMAP/SMTP (including switching an existing Gmail IMAP setup); Proton Mail needs Bridge. Calendar uses a separate Google Calendar connection. Contacts support CSV/vCard transfer plus read-only Apple **Mac Contacts** on macOS, with no ongoing Google Contacts or CardDAV connection. macOS is regularly tested; Android covers ARM64 devices running Android 9+; Windows and Linux builds are experimental. See [Compatibility and Limitations](compatibility.html).
