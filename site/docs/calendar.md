## Connect Google Calendar

Calendar connects separately from mail and works alongside Gmail or IMAP accounts.

1. Open **Settings → Calendar Accounts**.
2. Click **Connect Calendar** and sign in to Google in your browser.
3. Under **Calendars shown in the sidebar**, choose which calendars to show and pick their colors.

Google Calendar is the only calendar provider today. CalDAV is not yet supported.

## Today's Schedule

<ts-shot scene="today-schedule" alt="Today's schedule beside the inbox, with free time marked against working hours."></ts-shot>

Press <kbd>t</kbd> to show today's schedule beside your mail, and <kbd>-</kbd> or <kbd>=</kbd> to move between days. Set your timezone and working hours in **Settings → Availability**.

To share free times, use **Find a time** and **Check Schedule**, choose a date and duration, then pick the slots you want and click **Draft Reply With Selected Times**. When a draft is already open, the button inserts the times into it.

## Plan the Week

Press <kbd>2</kbd> for the Calendar view. Choose **Day**, **3-Day**, **Workweek**, or **Week**. ThreeStrands remembers your choice. **Today** jumps back to the current date, and **Calendars** shows or hides individual calendars.

<ts-shot scene="calendar-week" alt="A workweek calendar view across several Google calendars."></ts-shot>

## Create and Edit Events

Drag across the grid or click **New Event**. Add a title, times, the calendar, people to invite, and a description, then click **Create event** or press <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + Enter</kbd>.

Open an event you manage to edit or delete it. Guests are notified of changes.

## Answer Invitations

Open an event and choose **Yes**, **No**, or **Maybe** under **Going?**.

On macOS, you can make ThreeStrands the app that opens `.ics` invitation files in **Settings → Default Apps**. An opened invitation shows whether it's already on your calendar, lets you answer it, or offers **Add to Calendar**.
