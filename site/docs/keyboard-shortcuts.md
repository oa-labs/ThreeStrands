Single-letter shortcuts work whenever you're reading and no text field has focus; press <kbd>Escape</kbd> to leave an editing or search field and return to keyboard navigation. Press <kbd>?</kbd> in the app to see this list, or <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + k</kbd> to search commands and open [notes](notes.html#find-a-note) by name. On macOS, <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> means <kbd>⌘</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd> means <kbd>Option</kbd>; on Linux and Windows it means <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd>.

Press <kbd>h</kbd> in Mail to snooze a conversation (Remind Me), and <kbd>b</kbd> in Mail, **Today**, or Calendar to create an event.

<kbd>0</kbd> opens Today, and <kbd>1</kbd> through <kbd>7</kbd> open Mail, Calendar, Tasks, Notes, Contacts, Files, and Agents, in the sidebar's order. <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + 1</kbd> through <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + 9</kbd> switch between connected mail accounts in their Settings order, and <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + 0</kbd> shows **All accounts**.

Shortcuts follow the current view: <kbd>Shift + n</kbd> creates an event in Calendar or **Today**, a contact in Contacts, or a note in Notes; <kbd>s</kbd> stars in Mail, Notes, or Files, and schedules an item in **Today**. In Calendar and **Today**, <kbd>t</kbd> returns to today, and in Notes it opens today's note; elsewhere it switches the right column between the conversation’s details and the Schedule. <kbd>-</kbd> and <kbd>=</kbd> step through days in Calendar, **Today**, and daily notes in Notes.

In **Agents**, press <kbd>j</kbd>/<kbd>k</kbd> or <kbd>↓</kbd>/<kbd>↑</kbd> to move between Inbox Assistants, <kbd>e</kbd> to toggle enabled state, <kbd>n</kbd> to create a new assistant, <kbd>r</kbd> to run selected, <kbd>x</kbd> to test, and <kbd>o</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open details.

Press <kbd>\\</kbd> to hide the side panel in Mail, Contacts, Notes, Files, Tasks, or Calendar, and again to bring it back. Each view remembers whether you hid it.

In Mail, archive, trash, spam, read, star, snooze, and label shortcuts act on checked conversations when any are checked. <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + a</kbd> selects the visible conversations when focus is in the mail list (including **Drafts** and **Outbox**), and <kbd>Escape</kbd> clears the selection. Selecting text in an email or editing field keeps its normal behavior.

Outside editing fields, <kbd>z</kbd> or <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + z</kbd> undoes the latest eligible mail action, task completion, or pending send. Editing fields keep normal text undo. In Contacts, <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + Enter</kbd> saves your contact edits.
