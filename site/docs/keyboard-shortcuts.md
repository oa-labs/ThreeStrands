Single-letter shortcuts work whenever you're reading and no text field has focus. Press <kbd>?</kbd> in the app to see this list, or <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + k</kbd> to search every command by name. On macOS, <kbd>⌘/Ctrl</kbd> means <kbd>⌘</kbd>; on Linux and Windows it means <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>.

Press <kbd>h</kbd> in Mail to snooze a conversation (Remind Me), and <kbd>b</kbd> in Mail or Calendar to create an event.

<kbd>0</kbd> opens Today, and <kbd>1</kbd> through <kbd>7</kbd> open Mail, Calendar, Tasks, Notes, Contacts, Files, and Agents, in the sidebar's order.

Shortcuts follow the current view: <kbd>Shift + n</kbd> creates an event in Calendar, a contact in Contacts, or a note in Notes, and <kbd>s</kbd> stars a file in Files. In Calendar, <kbd>t</kbd> returns to today, and in Notes it opens today's note; elsewhere it switches the right column between the conversation’s details and the Schedule. <kbd>-</kbd> and <kbd>=</kbd> step through days in Calendar and through daily notes in Notes.

Press <kbd>\\</kbd> to hide the side panel in Mail, Contacts, Notes, Files, Tasks, or Calendar, and again to bring it back. Each view remembers whether you hid it.

In Mail, archive, trash, spam, read, star, snooze, and label shortcuts act on checked conversations when any are checked. <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + a</kbd> selects the visible conversations when focus is in the mail list, and <kbd>Escape</kbd> clears the selection. Selecting text in an email or editing field keeps its normal behavior.

Outside editing fields, <kbd>z</kbd> or <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + z</kbd> undoes the latest eligible mail action, task completion, or pending send. Editing fields keep normal text undo. In Contacts, <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + Enter</kbd> saves your contact edits.
