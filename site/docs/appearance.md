## Appearance and Reading {#appearance-and-reading}

Open **Settings → Appearance** for theme, accent color, font, text size, and when conversations are marked read. Press <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + d</kbd> to switch light and dark mode. Appearance settings stay on each computer.

## Enlarge Email {#enlarge-email}

**Email Zoom** enlarges email text, images, and spacing together. **Minimum Email Font Size** raises only smaller text before zoom is applied. It can change line breaks and layout; text inside images is unchanged. Start with zoom if you want to preserve the sender's proportions.

## Adjust Email Colors and Images {#adjust-email-colors-and-images}

**Email Text Colors** can improve hard-to-read sender colors or replace them with theme colors. Remote images remain controlled separately by [Privacy settings](privacy.html#remote-images). Loading an image will not make text inside it respond to font-size settings.

## Move Without a Mouse {#move-without-a-mouse}

Use <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + k</kbd> for commands and <kbd>?</kbd> for shortcuts. Single-letter shortcuts apply outside editing fields. <kbd>F6</kbd> moves between the composer and its side panel. Standard editing shortcuts keep their text-editing behavior inside fields.

## Choose Your Starting View {#choose-your-starting-view}

**Start With** can open Today when the app starts. Otherwise ThreeStrands remembers the workspace you last used. Today needs a connected calendar only for its agenda; mail and tasks work without one.
