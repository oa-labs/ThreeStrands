## Appearance and Reading {#appearance-and-reading}

Open **Settings → Appearance** for theme, accent color, font, and text size. Press <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + d</kbd> to switch light and dark mode. Appearance settings stay on each computer.

For when conversations are marked read, see [Reading & Triage](triage.html#mark-as-read).

## Enlarge Email {#enlarge-email}

**Email Zoom** enlarges email text, images, and spacing together. **Minimum Email Font Size** raises only smaller text before zoom is applied. It can change line breaks and layout; text inside images is unchanged. Start with zoom if you want to preserve the sender's proportions.

## Adjust Email Colors and Images {#adjust-email-colors-and-images}

**Email Text Colors** can improve hard-to-read sender colors or replace them with theme colors. Remote images remain controlled separately by [Privacy settings](privacy.html#remote-images). Loading an image will not make text inside it respond to font-size settings.

## Move Without a Mouse {#move-without-a-mouse}

Use <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + k</kbd> for commands and <kbd>?</kbd> for shortcuts. Single-letter shortcuts apply outside editing fields. <kbd>⌘/Ctrl + Shift + P</kbd> (or <kbd>F6</kbd>) moves between the composer and its side panel. Standard editing shortcuts keep their text-editing behavior inside fields.

## Add a Backdrop to Today {#today-backdrop}

**Today Backdrop**, under **Settings → Appearance**, is off until you turn it on. It draws a soft sky in a header across the top of [Today](today.html), with a matching tint around its panels that fades down the page: daytime from 6 am, twilight from 6 pm, and a navy night sky with stars and the moon from 9 pm. It is decoration only: it never sits behind your lists, text stays readable in light and dark themes, and with reduced motion on the scene doesn't fade in. It stays on this computer and isn't part of exported settings.

## Choose Your Starting View {#choose-your-starting-view}

**Start With** can open Today when the app starts. Otherwise ThreeStrands remembers the workspace you last used. Today needs a connected calendar only for its agenda; mail and tasks work without one.
